Campus Revision Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

1. Introduction

Campus Revision is an educational application designed to help students access revision materials, past examination papers, PDF documents, summaries, and other study resources. This policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and shared when you use the application.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, Campus Revision may process:

  • Account information: Information such as your email address, account identifier, authentication details, and profile information that you provide during registration or sign-in.
  • Educational information: Your selected university, school, department, programme, academic year, semester, and related revision preferences.
  • App activity: Information needed to provide revision materials, track usage allowances, manage subscriptions, and maintain account sessions.
  • Payment information: Transaction references, payment status, and related subscription information needed to process and verify payments. M-Pesa or other payment providers may independently process information under their own privacy policies.
  • Files and content: Documents or other files you choose to upload when using supported features, including service requests.
  • Advertising and device information: Advertising identifiers and device or app information may be processed by advertising providers when advertising features are used.
  • Communications: Information you provide when contacting us or submitting a support request.

The precise information processed depends on the features you use and the permissions you grant.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Authenticate users and help protect accounts against unauthorised access.
  • Provide educational materials, past papers, and revision tools.
  • Provide AI-assisted summaries or other study features when requested.
  • Manage usage allowances, subscriptions, and payment verification.
  • Display and measure advertisements where applicable.
  • Respond to support requests and service enquiries.
  • Maintain, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the application.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

4. Third-Party Services

Campus Revision uses or may use third-party services to provide its functionality. Depending on the feature, these may include:

  • Google Firebase: Services such as Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and Firebase Storage may be used for account management, data storage, and file storage.
  • Google AdMob: Advertising services may process device identifiers, advertising interactions, and related information to deliver and measure advertisements, subject to your settings and applicable consent requirements.
  • AI service providers: Text or document content submitted for AI-assisted features may be transmitted to the configured AI provider to generate a response.
  • M-Pesa and payment service providers: Transaction details may be processed to initiate, verify, and record payments.

These providers process information under their applicable terms, security practices, and privacy policies. Information is shared only as needed for the relevant service, as otherwise disclosed, or as required by law.

5. AI Features

If you use AI-assisted features, relevant text or document content may be sent to an external AI service for processing. Avoid submitting passwords, payment credentials, or other sensitive personal information in AI prompts or uploaded documents unless it is strictly necessary and you understand how that information will be processed.

6. Data Storage and Security

Information may be stored locally on your device and on third-party infrastructure used by Campus Revision. We take reasonable steps to protect information against unauthorised access, loss, alteration, or disclosure. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the application, maintain accounts and transaction records, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce applicable agreements. Retention periods may differ by information type and service provider.

You may request deletion of your account and associated personal information by contacting us using the details below. Some information may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate security and transaction-record purposes.

8. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. You can also manage applicable device permissions and advertising preferences through your device settings. Contact us to make a privacy-related request.

9. Children's Privacy

Campus Revision is intended for its declared target audience and is not knowingly designed to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. Where parental consent or other safeguards are legally required, those requirements must be followed. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately.

10. International Processing

Some service providers may process or store information in countries outside your country of residence. Where required, appropriate safeguards should be applied to such transfers.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy when the application, its services, or applicable legal requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised update date.
